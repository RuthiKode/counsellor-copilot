What to say

A three-line slip for the counsellor, built only from what the lead told us and the reasons behind its score.

These slips use a fixed template. With an API key the Python app can write them with Claude, and code rejects any line that cites something not in the lead's data.

Five kinds of lead

Groups found by clustering behaviour and profile. They explain who the leads are; the score still decides the order within each group.

How it works, and why the numbers are honest

Leaving out what counsellors write after a call

Nine columns, like call notes and quality ratings, only exist after a counsellor has spoken to the lead. With them in, the model looks far better than it could ever be on a new lead.

What the results recommend

  • Data. 9,240 leads from one edtech company (Kaggle, X Education). Every number on this page comes from 2,310 of them the model never trained on.
  • Model. Logistic regression with calibrated probabilities. LightGBM was tried and fell short of the gain needed to give up explainability.
  • Limits. Past data from one company, assumed prices, and no record of what calling changes. These are projections, not measured increases.
