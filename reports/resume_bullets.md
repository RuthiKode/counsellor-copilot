# Resume bullets (XYZ form)

All numbers are measured on 2,310 held-out test leads (retrospective simulation), so the wording is
"would capture" / "identified", never "increased".

**Project line (put above either bullet)**

Counsellor Copilot: lead scoring and call planning | Live: [ruthikode.github.io/counsellor-copilot](https://ruthikode.github.io/counsellor-copilot/) | Code: [github.com/RuthiKode/counsellor-copilot](https://github.com/RuthiKode/counsellor-copilot)

**Version A — analytics / model focus**
- Built a calibrated lead-scoring model for an edtech admissions funnel that would let counsellors capture
  43% of enrolments by calling the top 20% of leads (2.1× the 38.5% baseline; 1.22 vs 2.60 calls per
  enrolment on 2,310 held-out of 9,240 leads), by auditing and removing 9 post-contact leakage columns that
  had inflated test AUC from 0.85 to 0.98.

**Version B — business / growth focus**
- Identified a call-prioritisation policy that would reach 77% of enrolments with 1,000 calls vs 43% in
  random order (~304 more enrolments per 2,310 leads, ₹27.3 lakh at assumed prices), by turning calibrated
  lead scores into a profit-based call cutoff, 5 lead personas with playbooks, and a live interactive
  planner website ([ruthikode.github.io/counsellor-copilot](https://ruthikode.github.io/counsellor-copilot/))
  backed by a Streamlit app with guard-railed AI call briefs.
